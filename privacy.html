Privacy & Terms

Last updated: May 2025
The short version

We collect only what we need. We never sell your data. Respondent identities are never revealed. You can delete your profile and all associated data at any time.

1. What Data We Collect

Data Why we collect it Who sees it
Your name, age, city, job To build your profile and roast card Anyone you share your link with
Your photo (optional) To display on your profile Anyone you share your link with
Respondent email address To prevent duplicate responses No Filter Zim only — never the profile owner
Anonymous responses To generate your AI roast card AI only — never attributed to respondent
Push notification token To send you response alerts No Filter Zim only
Roast scores For leaderboard rankings Public leaderboard

2. The Anonymity Guarantee

This is the most important part of our privacy policy. We make one unconditional guarantee: respondent identities are never revealed to profile owners. Not now. Not ever.

We collect respondent email addresses only to prevent the same person from submitting multiple responses. This email is stored securely and is never displayed, shared, or sold to anyone including the profile owner.

Even if a profile owner requests to know who responded, we will not provide this information. Even if compelled by a third party we will notify respondents before disclosing any information where legally permissible.

3. How We Store Your Data

All data is stored securely on Supabase, a PostgreSQL database provider with enterprise-grade security. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We use Row Level Security policies to ensure users can only access their own data.

Profile photos are stored as base64 encoded strings in our database. We do not use third party image hosting services.

4. Google Sign In

Respondents sign in with Google to prevent duplicate responses. We receive your Google email address and display name only. We do not access your Google contacts, calendar, drive, or any other Google data.

Your Google sign in information is used solely for authentication and duplicate prevention. It is never shared with profile owners.

5. AI Processing

Anonymous responses are sent to OpenAI's API to generate roast cards and villain arc plans. Responses are sent without any identifying information. OpenAI processes this data according to their own privacy policy. We do not use your data to train AI models.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

— Delete your profile and all associated data at any time from your profile page

— Request a copy of all data we hold about you

— Correct any inaccurate information on your profile

— Opt out of push notifications at any time in your browser settings

— Request removal from the public leaderboard

To exercise any of these rights contact us at privacy@nofilter.co.zw or use the delete option on your profile page.

7. Data We Never Collect

— We never collect payment card details directly (handled by payment processor)

— We never collect your phone number

— We never track your location beyond the city you enter in your profile

— We never sell your data to advertisers or third parties

— We never use your data to train AI models

8. Cookies

We use localStorage in your browser to store your profile data locally for faster loading. We do not use tracking cookies or third party analytics cookies. We do not use advertising cookies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We will notify users of significant changes to this privacy policy via push notification before changes take effect. Continued use of No Filter Zim after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For privacy related requests contact us at: privacy@nofilter.co.zw

11. Terms of Service

No Filter Zim is a platform for anonymous honest feedback. By using this service you agree that responses may be direct, critical, and emotionally challenging. You also agree not to use the platform to harass or target other people, and to respect the anonymity of respondents.

You must be 18 years or older to create a profile. Continued use of No Filter Zim after updates constitutes acceptance of these terms.

Roast cards, villain arc plans, and playlists are generated by AI and are for entertainment and personal growth only. This is not professional advice.

Respondent email addresses are collected only to prevent duplicate responses and are never shared with profile owners.

No Filter Zim may remove any content or profiles that violate these terms without notice. We reserve the right to update these terms at any time.

For questions about privacy or terms contact privacy@nofilter.co.zw.
